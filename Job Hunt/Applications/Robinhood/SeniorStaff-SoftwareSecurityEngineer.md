### **About the Role & Team**

At Robinhood, security is foundational to our mission of democratizing finance for all. As a Senior Staff Software Security Engineer on our Security Engineering team, you will serve as the most senior individual contributor across a ~40-person organization spanning Infrastructure Security, Cloud Security, Cryptography, Access Management Platform (IAM), AI Security, and our Detection & Response platform. You will bridge domain expertise, set long-term technical direction, and build the security architecture that enables Robinhood to innovate safely, including the foundation for emerging AI, agentic experiences, and production AI capabilities.

This is an autonomous, highly visible role where you will identify high-impact technical problems, bring teams together around a unified strategy, and drive major cross-cutting initiatives from idea through execution.

### **What You'll Do**

- **Define Technical Strategy:** Provide cross-domain technical strategy and architectural direction across Security Engineering, connecting distinct security domains into a cohesive strategy.
- **Build AI Security Foundations:** Help establish and evolve Robinhood’s security foundation for emerging AI technologies, agentic use cases, internal automation, and production AI applications.
- **Drive Strategic Initiatives:** Identify high-leverage technical problems across infrastructure, cloud, identity, and detection systems, driving major cross-cutting initiatives from concept through production.
- **Build & Ship**: Write code and land production systems for the hardest problems you identify, from prototype through operation, rather than handing designs to other teams.
- **Partner & Align:** Work closely with engineering managers and senior technical leaders to establish quarterly priorities, resolve complex technical trade-offs, and align roadmaps across teams.
- **Elevate the Bar:** Raise the technical bar across the organization through direct mentorship, technical leadership, and hands-on architectural guidance for engineers.
- **Maintain Operational Proximity:** Remain technically close to production environments to ensure security strategies and architectural decisions remain grounded in operational reality.
