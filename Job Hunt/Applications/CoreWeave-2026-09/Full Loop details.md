## Domain-Specific Assessment (1 hour)

This round assesses your technical depth in systems design with a strong lens on IAM and security. Because this is an IAM role, be ready to go deeper on identity, authentication, and authorization.

Come prepared to:

- Explain your reasoning step by step and ask clarifying questions
- Speak to IAM-specific concerns (authn/authz, identity lifecycle, access control models)
- Discuss API security — be ready to go deep on authentication and authorization mechanisms
- Demonstrate familiarity with standards: OIDC, OAuth, SAML, JWT, SCIM
- Demonstrate familiarity with Authorization models: RBAC, ABAC, ReBAC etc

## System Design Interview (1 hour)

A classical system design interview that starts with a focused engineering challenge and expands in scope. You'll be evaluated on your ability to design a resilient, high-scale distributed system — with particular attention to how you handle failure, concurrency, data consistency, and partial outages across data centers.

Come prepared to:
- Ask clarifying questions before diving in — scope matters here
- Think out loud and reason through trade-offs explicitly
- Address failure scenarios: what breaks, why, and how you'd recover
- Connect technical decisions to real-world business and operational impact
- There's no single right answer — they want to see how you navigate complexity

## Product Partnership (30 minutes)

This conversation focuses on your history leading large-scale, long-duration projects — not just technical depth. Be ready to speak to how you've driven alignment across teams, navigated ambiguity, and delivered outcomes that outlasted your direct involvement.

Come prepared to:

- Walk through a project where you influenced direction without direct authority
- Discuss a time you had to make a hard call under uncertainty or pressure
- Share how you've balanced short-term delivery with longer-term architectural thinking

### General 

Across every round, keep these in mind:
- Ask clarifying questions before diving in — interviewers expect and appreciate this
- Think out loud: your reasoning matters as much as your conclusions
- Keep the customer experience front and center; know who the customer is and talk through how your choices impact them
- Tie your technical decisions back to real impact — reliability, security posture, developer experience, compliance
- It's okay not to have all the answers. How you work through uncertainty is what we're most interested in
- This role sits at the intersection of security, scale, and cross-functional influence — weave that context into your answers wherever it fits naturally
