This round is focused on how you operate as a Staff level engineer and about your judgment, influence, and leadership.

**What to expect:** the hiring manager will be interested in examples of how you've

1. Navigated ambiguous or competing requirements and landed on a clear direction.
2. Built alignment across Product, Security, Platform, and leadership without formal authority.
3. Owned complex, high stakes work from start to finish.
4. Brought a security first, operationally mature lens to your decisions.
5. Mentored and raised the level of the engineers around you.

**How to prepare:** it helps to come with two or three concrete stories you can provide depth and detail on. What the situation was, how you approached it, and what you'd do differently today.
