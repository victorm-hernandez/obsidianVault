# Reasons for leaving each previous position

## Architect, Docusign

Following a change in division leadership under a new VP, the company underwent a strategic restructuring that included layoffs. As a recent hire, my position was unfortunately impacted by this reduction in force.

## Tech Lead, Microsoft

I transitioned to pursue an opportunity for significant professional growth. I was offered a Software Architect role where I was responsible for the technical direction and strategy of an entire Identity division, spanning five engineering teams.

## Tech Lead, Salesforce

A former manager recruited me for a highly compelling opportunity to lead a bare-metal, foundational identity provider. Because this system underpinned all Azure infrastructure.I recognized it as an exceptional career opportunity and made the transition.

## Senior Engineer, Microsoft

A former manager reached out regarding a role at this company to build a new identity provider from the ground up. The objective was to centralize an identity strategy that had become highly fragmented across different technology stacks and workflows due to multiple historical acquisitions. Because greenfield identity projects of this scale are exceptionally rare in the industry, I decided to pursue the challenge.

## Technical Lead, Hewlett-Packard

I was recruited by Microsoft for a Software engineer position. While my previous job was challenging, joining a world-class engineering organization to build a core, main-line product was the ideal next step in my career trajectory.

## CFE

While I valued my time at CFE, their core business wasn't technology, which naturally limited the project scope. Having maximized my professional growth within that single domain, I transitioned to a tech-first organization to pursue broader architectural challenges.

## ReactiveOne

I co-founded this company so this was a tough decision. I was recruited by Microsoft for a Software engineer position. Joining a world-class engineering organization to build a core, main-line product was the ideal next step in my career trajectory.

## Microtec

While this startup offered valuable hands-on experience in full-stack development and client management, the project scale was naturally constrained by the company's size. I transitioned to a larger enterprise to take on broader, more complex technical challenges.
