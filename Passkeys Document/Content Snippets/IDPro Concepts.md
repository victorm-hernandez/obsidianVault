
## IDPro

**IDPro** is a global, open, non-profit professional association established to support, define, and elevate the digital identity profession.  

- **Purpose:** It acts as a collective voice for IAM practitioners, security experts, and privacy professionals. It bridges the gap between technical execution and business strategy, offering a vendor-neutral space for collaboration, networking, and mentorship.


## CIDPRO

**CIDPRO** stands for **Certified Identity Professional**. It is the flagship professional certification program created and administered by IDPro.  

- **Purpose:** Designed by practitioners for practitioners, it provides a rigorous, standardized way to validate foundational IAM knowledge and industry experience.  
    
- **Core Domains:** The certification exam tests proficiency across several critical areas of identity management, including:  
    ⚬ Basic elements of an identity solution  
    ⚬ Identifiers, identity lifecycle, and governance  
    ⚬ Authentication, authorization, and federation  
    ⚬ Security architectures, rules, standards, and privacy frameworks  
    
- **Target Audience:** It serves both individual professionals looking to benchmark and prove their expertise, and employers seeking to establish baseline proficiencies for their engineering and security teams.

A comprehensive, living collection of vendor-neutral reference materials and articles covering foundational and advanced IAM topics.
