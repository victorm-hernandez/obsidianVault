### How to Join the Group

Your application is subject to approval by the Chairs and Staff Contacts of this group. Please contact [Simone Onofri](mailto:simone@w3.org) to discuss your intent to complete an application before starting the process.

1. Read the [Web Authentication Working Group Charter](https://www.w3.org/groups/wg/webauthn/charters/active/).
2. Read the [Invited Expert agreement](https://www.w3.org/invited-experts/agreement/).
3. Read the [Policy for Approval of Invited Experts](https://www.w3.org/invited-experts/).
4. Create a [W3C account](https://www.w3.org/account/request/) if you don't already have one.
5. Complete a [W3C Invited Expert Application](https://www.w3.org/groups/wg/webauthn/apply-as-invited-expert/). You should receive a reply within **ten (10) business days**. If your request is accepted, you will receive an invitation with additional instructions for formally joining the group.
6. You will then be able to complete the [form for joining and participating in the Web Authentication Working Group](https://www.w3.org/groups/wg/webauthn/join/) as an Invited Expert, at which point you may begin your participation in the group.

**Note**: All actions carried out through this system are reported by email to the relevant parties; copies are sent to w3c-archive@w3.org ([Member-readable archive](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Member/w3c-archive/)).
