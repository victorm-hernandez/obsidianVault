

This repository represents the **IDPro Body of Knowledge**, providing enterprise IAM standards, terminology, and operational guidance.

- `Workforce IAM/authentication-methods.md`
    - **Topic:** Small section on Passkeys and passwordless methods in Workforce IAM. Outlines public/private key cryptography, device-bound vs. synced passkeys, biometrics, and FIDO2 integration.
- `Digital Identity/idpro-account-recovery-final.md`
    - **Topic:** Account recovery strategies in modern identity systems. Explains how synced passkeys (1Password, Dashlane, Apple/Google) simplify recovery via provider ecosystems, and how to handle device-bound hardware passkey replacement.
- `CIAM/intro-to-ciam.md`
    - **Topic:** Overview of Customer Identity and Access Management. Brief section about passwordless authentication, the W3C WebAuthn standard, and consumer adoption of passkeys.
- `Introduction/AuthN-and-AuthZ.md`
    - **Topic:** Core authentication and authorization concepts. Tiny reference to this credential type.
- `Introduction/Intro-to-Identity-2.md`
    - **Topic:** Decoupled identity architecture, highlighting how FIDO2 (W3C WebAuthn and CTAP2) separates identity binding from credential verification.
- `Digital Identity/practical-implications-PKI.md`
    - **Topic:** Practical applications of Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), explaining how passkeys and Verifiable Credentials utilize asymmetric cryptography.
- `Digital Identity/idpro-bok-incident-response-framework.md`
    - **Topic:** Incident response framework referencing passwordless authentication and passkeys as target security controls in enterprise incident management.
- `terminology.md`
    - **Topic:** Standard identity glossary providing formal industry definitions for **Passkeys**, **FIDO2 Security Keys**, **Passwordless Authentication**, and **Biometric** verification.
