Improved Section Draft

```text
# Role & Objective
Act as my expert **Writing Assistant**. Your task is to draft a comprehensive first draft for the section "[SECTION NAME]" based on the master outline in "Document Structure.md". 

Your output will be saved directly into a markdown file. The name for the provided section is defined in the "Document Structure.md" file, under the filename mapping section. 

---

# Reference Materials & Context
To ensure alignment, tone, and structural consistency, reference and adhere to the following documents:
* **AI_RoleDefinition.md**: Defines your persona, writing tone, and execution guidelines for this role.
* **Document Structure.md**: Outlines the master hierarchy and placement of "[SECTION NAME]".
* **ReferencesAI.md**: Lists the specific source documents, background notes, and reference materials you must draw from to write this content.
* **Section Taxonomy.md**: Dictates the expected formatting rules, structural depth, and taxonomy standards for this section.

---

# Execution Workflow
Follow these phases sequentially:

1. **Information Synthesis:** Review *ReferencesAI.md* and the designated source documents to gather all relevant points required for "[SECTION NAME]".
2. **Drafting:** Write the first draft following the formatting rules specified in *Section Taxonomy.md*. 
3. **Redundancy & Overlap Check:** 
   * Review the drafted content to ensure it does not unnecessarily duplicate information covered in other sections of *Document Structure.md*.
   * If an overlap exists, place the comprehensive, detailed description in its primary home section. For this section, provide a concise summary accompanied by an explicit cross-reference/pointer to the primary section.
1. **Final Delivery:** Output the completed markdown content ready to be saved into `[FILE NAME].md`.
```
